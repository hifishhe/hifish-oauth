Privacy Policy — Hermes Wukong

Hermes Wukong is a private personal automation tool operated by Yu He.

The application accesses Gmail only with read-only permission to scan the account owner’s email for messages that may need attention. Email data is processed and stored only in the owner’s private automation environment. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

The account owner can revoke access at any time from their Google Account security settings.
